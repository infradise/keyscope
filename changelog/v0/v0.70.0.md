# Changelog

## v0.70.0

This release introduces the **Keyscope Workspace Theme Engine** alongside **40 curated workspace color themes** (20 Dark and 20 Light palettes paired across 4 distinct design categories: **Classic**, **Modern**, **Artisan**, and **Retro**). Rather than merely swapping button accent colors, each theme re-engineers the 4-layer surface luminance hierarchy, paper and carbon materiality, ambient color temperature, and dynamic foreground contrast across every workspace and toolkit in Keyscope.

### **Workspace Theme Engine** with **40 Curated Workspace Color Themes**

- **Workspace Theme Engine & Appearance Controls (`Settings > Theme`)**:
  - **3-Way Appearance Mode**: Added a segmented control supporting **Dark**, **Light**, and **System** (real-time OS appearance synchronization) modes, dynamically pairing each preset's dark and light counterparts.
  - **Category Filter & Badge Bar**: Organized all 20 dual-mode theme presets into 4 curated categories (**Classic 5**, **Modern 5**, **Artisan 5**, **Retro 5**, and **All 20**) for effortless browsing and future extensibility.

### **Text Editor (Toolkit)**

* **Multi-Buffer Text Editor & Scratchpad**: Added a built-in **Text Editor** to the Dev Toolkit (accessible from both the bottom toolkit panel and full-screen view) for jotting down notes, K8s manifests, payloads, and scratch buffers without leaving the IDE.
